Balnur Assan  ·  Farabi University, Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics, Department of Mechanics

Perturbative Methods in Studying the Secular Evolution of Many Body Problem with Variable Masses

The classical many body problem with constant masses is non-integrable, therefore, to investigate the dynamics of planetary systems, perturbation theory based on the exact solution of the two-body problem is usually applied. However, in the case of variable masses, the two-body problem is generally non-integrable as well. Consequently, to apply perturbation theory, another integrable model problem is used as the unperturbed motion, the solutions of which are close to the solution of the two-body problem with variable masses (in particular, when the mass changes according to Meshchersky's first law, the solutions coincide exactly). In this talk, perturbation theory is used to derive the differential equations determining the orbital elements of the relative motion of one body around another. Here, the models of perturbed aperiodic motion along a conic section and along a quasi-conic section are considered and compared. It is shown that the model based on aperiodic motion along a quasi-conic section is more suitable for generalization to the many body problem with variable masses. 14 November 2026, 14:30-15:30 Aula D'Antoni
Shri Lal Raghudev Ram Singh  ·  University of Waterloo

Uniform stabilization on general Banach spaces using perturbation theory for quasi-compact semigroups

The study of stability and stabilization of infinite-dimensional dynamical systems plays an important role in the analysis and control of partial differential equations. Semigroup theory provides a powerful abstract framework for studying the long-term behaviour of such systems, while perturbation theory allows us to understand how their stability properties are affected by changes in the underlying dynamics. In this talk, we study uniform exponential stabilization for strongly continuous semigroups on general Banach spaces through a perturbation theory for quasi-compact semigroups. We first look at the result which states that quasi-compactness is preserved under suitable θ-subordinate perturbations of generators of analytic semigroups, provided that the perturbation is compact along the trajectories of the unperturbed semigroup. Building on this preservation result, we establish sufficient conditions under which uniform exponential stability is preserved under θ-subordinate perturbations on arbitrary Banach spaces. Our approach exploits the spectral structure of quasi-compact semigroups and the relationship between quasi-compactness and the asymptotic stability properties of strongly continuous semigroups.
